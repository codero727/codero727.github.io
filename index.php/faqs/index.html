About Dr. Luna

Do you accept insurance?

Dr Luna’s practice is fully out-of-network and is not in-network with any commercial insurance plans, but can provide a bill of services to you for submission to your insurance for reimbursement.

What are your fees?

Medication Management (30 min follow up, typically monthly): $350 per session.

Psychotherapy +/- Medication Management (45 min follow up, typically weekly): $525 per session.

Initial Evaluations: $600 per 45m session. For initial evaluations, I require two 45m sessions for adults or three 45m sessions for children/adolescents (parents, child/adolescents, joint) before accepting you into my practice.

Consultations/Second opinions: $600 per 45m session.

Do you treat adults?

Yes. Dr Luna’s training encompassed working with both adults and children/adolescents, which makes him especially suited to support those whose challenges began earlier in life or who are navigating issues related to development, family dynamics, or parenting.

Do you offer virtual or in-person services?

At this time, Dr Luna’s practice offers only virtual services using secure HIPAA compliant telecommunication platforms for individuals residing in New York.

I’d like to discuss further. What’s the next step?
